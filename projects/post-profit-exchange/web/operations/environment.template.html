Cash after reserve and unpaid costs = cash − reserve − unpaid costs. A negative amount shows a shortfall.

Net assets = cash + stock at purchase cost − unpaid costs.

Earned funding tracks sales income minus the purchase cost of goods sold and recorded expenses, added to its opening balance. It is not extra cash.
