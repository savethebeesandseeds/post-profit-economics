    Record activity

    Record a completed sale, a paid delivery or a new expense. Saving updates the accounts and stock.

    Record a sale

    Product sold from this operation’s catalog. Selecting a product loads its saved public price; enter the price actually received for this sale.

    Number of whole units sold. The application checks that enough stock is available. Fractional units are not supported.

    Enter the actual price received.

    Actual amount received for one unit. It starts at the saved public price. Changing it here affects only this sale; it does not change the public price. Use up to two decimal places, for example 2.50.

    Record a paid delivery

    Product received from this operation’s catalog. Each delivery adds a separate stock lot with its own purchase cost.

    Number of whole units received. The total purchase payment is this quantity multiplied by the purchase cost per unit.

    Actual purchase cost of one unit in this delivery. The total is deducted from recorded cash. Zero is allowed for a free receipt. This cost does not update the saved replacement cost or public price.

    Delivery reference

    Unique reference for this batch of stock, generated automatically. You may replace it with 1–64 letters, digits, hyphens, underscores or dots. Purchase costs remain attached to the batch as its units are sold.

    Add a new expense

    Choose worker pay for wages or operating expense for other running costs. Both reduce the earned funding balance once the expense is recorded.

    Already paid deducts the amount from cash. Still to be paid creates an unpaid obligation without changing cash. Both record a new expense; this form cannot settle an obligation that was recorded earlier.

    Short description identifying this new cost, such as the supplier, service or pay period. It is retained with the transaction for later inspection.

    Total amount of this new expense in the operation’s currency. Use a positive amount with up to two decimal places, for example 12.50.

    Date of the transaction. It must be on or after the current accounts date; the application does not insert records into an earlier accounting position.

    Enter a quantity to see the total.

    Full accounts

    Cash
    Cash recorded in the accounts, including the reserve. Deliveries, cash sales and paid expenses change this balance when saved; the application does not move money through a bank or payment provider.
    Reserve within cash
    Part of the cash balance protected from being spent on paid deliveries and expenses. It is already included in cash and must not be counted as additional money.
    Earned funding balance
    The opening policy balance, plus sales proceeds, minus the purchase cost of stock sold and new expenses, including unpaid expenses. It is a policy balance, not a separate cash account or automatically distributable profit.
    Inventory at acquisition cost
    Remaining units in each stock lot multiplied by that lot’s recorded purchase cost, added across all products. The oldest available lots are used first when sales are recorded.
    Outstanding obligations
    Recorded costs that remain unpaid. They are already accounted for as expenses. Entering them again as new expenses would count the cost twice.
    Cash excluding reserve
    Recorded cash minus the protected reserve. Unpaid obligations have not been deducted from this figure.
    Cash after obligations and reserve
    Recorded cash minus the protected reserve and unpaid obligations. It can be negative. It does not account for unrecorded or future costs.
    Net assets
    Recorded cash plus stock at purchase cost, minus unpaid obligations. The reserve remains part of cash and is not deducted again.

    Outstanding obligations

    RecordKindAmount
