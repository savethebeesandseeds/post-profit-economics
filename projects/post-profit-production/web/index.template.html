Dependencies · retained progress · declared risk

Each job instantiates a process. Its stages draw on input stocks and available pools, then produce configured outputs. The timeline emerges from those constraints.

What the model assumes
  • Inputs are consumed together at a feasible stage start. Outputs become available to other stages on the next tick.
  • Pauses retain work already completed. Available labor and capacity reset each tick.
  • FIFO and earliest deadline are greedy scheduling policies. They establish no optimum or fairness claim.
  • Risk probabilities are supplied assumptions and remain uncalibrated. A recorded reason is the first blocking condition.
  • Compensation accrues across the full horizon, including idle time and absence. Payment, funding, viability and governance are not modeled.
